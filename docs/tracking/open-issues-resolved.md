- **Foul/card heuristic issues ~7 red cards per 9 minutes — RESOLVED July 26, 2026 (§5.Z.9 foul & discipline balance pass)** — *opened July 26, 2026 (§5.Z Phase H landing).* A played match no longer empties itself: measured over one match-equivalent of composed play, **480 → 21.0 fouls, 147 → 3.0 yellows, 75 → 1.0 reds per 90 minutes** (football reference ~22 / ~3.5 / ~0.25), and no team dropped below eleven players where the pre-fix engine reduced teams to five to seven inside nine minutes. **The measurement pass refuted this entry's own diagnosis.** The framing above — a `[GT]` threshold question — assumed a threshold existed that would produce a football-plausible rate.
