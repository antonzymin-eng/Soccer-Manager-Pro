- **Conversion at contact — the CLAIM defect is FIXED (ERR-011-008); REMAINDER: the `pointQuality` lottery is blocked on a design decision, and parry placement is unfixed-but-currently-costless** — *opened August 3, 2026 (§5.Z.23).* §5.Z.22 recorded the goal-rate residual as *"marginal, end-of-envelope touches whose parries and spills keep the ball alive"* and named two levers. **The premise was refuted by measurement**: per-contact ball speed before → after is parried 10.8 → 0.0, deflected 10.3 → 4.2, spilled 13.9 → 9.0, missed 9.5 → 9.5 — and **caught 11.1 → 10.8** (one tick of drag). The parries and spills work; the CATCH did nothing, and 7 of 10 catches were followed by a goal within 5 s. **That half is closed** (ERR-011-008 — `ParkBall()` at both claim sites; goals 5.0 → 3.7/match). **What remains open:** (a) the Stage-0 **`pointQuality` lottery**, now quantified — quality 0.559 / 0.564 / 0.590 across *rising* contact marginality with catch rate 43% / 38% / **50%**, i.e. blind AND inverted, and `HandlingPointErrorSigmaM` provably cancels out of its own formula (σ·z ÷ σ), a `[GT]` dial whose value cannot matter at any setting. The geometry-aware form was **implemented and measured, then reverted**: it fixes the direction (0.261 / 0.255 / 0.150) and collapses the level to **zero catches and zero parries**, goals 3.7 → 4.3/match, because mean contact marginality is 0.68 and no `[GT]` inside #11 §3.4.5's ranges lifts the blend back over `CatchThreshold`'s 0.65 floor. **The ladder refuses, so the next action is an owner decision — improve contact geometry upstream (the §5.Z.22 commit gate's prediction error is what puts the ball at the envelope edge), or re-scale the divisor and widen the `[GT]` ranges — not a calibration run.** (b) **Parry placement**: `ComputeParryVelocity` reverses the incoming velocity and rotates it by a *deterministic* ≤ 0.20 rad, so a parry goes back out along the shot line toward the attackers; real keepers parry wide. It produced **zero goals** in both the pre- and post-fix corpora, so it is a realism defect with no current goal-rate cost — but it becomes load-bearing the moment (a) lands and pushes contacts out of the catch band. (c) A claimed ball is not held at hand height and the keeper cannot carry it (the parked ball settles under gravity); a genuine hand-carry needs the `BallStateType.Controlled` question answered first — that state has **zero production call sites** and no exit path. Full detail and tables: `docs/tracking/gk-conversion-at-contact-design.md` §4 and §7.

- **Close-chance creation — the bottleneck is the final-third → penalty-area transition (6.5% vs football's ~40%), NOT shot selection and NOT possession churn** — *opened August 3, 2026 (§5.Z.23); supersedes the framing §5.Z.21 recorded.* §5.Z.21's shot-volume ladder refused count ≈ 25 AND mean ≤ 22 m jointly and attributed the bound to "close-chance creation at ~3× football's final-third churn". It also **owns the residual shot-count gap** (13.7 vs football's ~25) that §5.Z.21 could not close by any falloff value.
