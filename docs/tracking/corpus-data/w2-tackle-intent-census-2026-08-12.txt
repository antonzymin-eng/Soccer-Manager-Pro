Counted in POSSESSION EPISODES, not strides: at 10 Hz a match is ~54 000
strides, so a stride count measures the sampling rate, not the football.

episodes : spells in which the OTHER team held the ball (this team defending)
<=3m     : of those, episodes where any outfielder of this team came within
           #14's own TackleEligibleRadiusM of the carrier
<=2m/1.5m: the same, at two tighter bands — the contact radius a wired tackle
           would use is a design decision this instrument informs, so the
           bands are reported rather than one being picked here
poolElig : of the <=3m episodes, those where the nearby man was HOLD_SHAPE-
           eligible — the only population #14 can produce tackle intent for
presser  : of the <=3m episodes, those where he carried a #13 press role.
           FR-DA-010 excludes exactly these from the pool, so an episode here
           and NOT in poolElig is a challenge the current design forbids
intent   : episodes with >=1 tackle intent naming the carrier
COMMIT   : of those, >=1 in Commit mode — the population a wired tackle,
           gated as W2 proposes, could act on at all
ballGap  : episodes where the CARRIER was ever >1 m from the ball he holds.
           Possession is a flag, not a kinematic constraint (backlog W6), so
           tackler-to-carrier and tackler-to-BALL are different distances

Reading it — a zero must arrive with its cause attached:
  * COMMIT == 0 and <=3m ~ 0  => nobody is ever near the man on the ball.
    W2 is dead UPSTREAM, at positioning; wiring the chain changes nothing.
  * COMMIT == 0, <=3m large, poolElig ~ 0, presser large => the only player
    near the carrier is the one #13 sent at him, and FR-DA-010 excludes him
    from the pool by construction. Then the tackle producer belongs in #13,
    or #14's pool exclusion is wrong for tackles — a SPEC question, not an
    engine one, and not something a call site may decide by itself.
  * COMMIT == 0, poolElig large, intent ~ 0 => MarkAssigner is not marking
    the carrier. It never reads the ball, so this is the expected shape.
  * intent large, COMMIT == 0 => the CoverageDepth floor or the last-man
    override is the bound; both are #14 [GT]s frozen under KD-W1.
