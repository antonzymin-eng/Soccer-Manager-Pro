Reading it:
  * commits > 0 and launches ≈ commits => the trigger fires AND the state
    machine acts on it. commits >> launches => intents are being committed
    from states that cannot launch, or disarmed before the next tick.
  * offLine large with maxOut small => the keeper is committing to targets
    he is already on top of; the trigger is arming on nothing.
  * a keeper stuck in Rushing shows as offLine climbing without new launches
    — that is ERR-011-009 regressing.
