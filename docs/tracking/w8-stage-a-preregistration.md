# W8 Stage A preregistration — frozen before baseline

Status: preregistered measurement design. Merge this document and `W8StageADiagnosticTests` together before dispatching `w8-stage-a` or reading any result-bearing six-seed output. The measurement artifact records its exact commit SHA; do not substitute a later tree without calling it a new arm. No W8 B wiring, numeric tuning, or #11 clock correction belongs in A.

## Population and exposure

Use `new MatchEngine(seed)`, `EnableGkHeading()`, no `ConfigureSquads` override, and 324,000 consecutive 60 Hz ticks per seed (90 simulated minutes). The ordered seeds are `0x0F1E2D3C4B5A6978`, `0x00000000D1A6D05E`, `0x0000000000000001`, `0x00000000ABCDEF12`, `0x0000000099887766`, `0x000000005A5A5A5A`. Report keeper ID, team ID, seed, and 90 keeper match minutes per seed; if a keeper is sent off, report eligible exposure separately before making a rate claim. Each seed finishes at full time; report terminal snapshot digest. Do not silently replace, trim, or discard a seed.

## Episode and event definitions

The opt-in observer is per engine instance, never serialized, never read by gameplay, consumes no RNG, and reports the current 60 Hz frame at each production call. A `hand` episode starts at #11's `SetPossessor` call (all three physical claim sites share the adapter). A `feet` episode starts when a keeper acquires possession without an immediately preceding hand claim. End at the first production ownership release, other-agent acquisition, or restart. An episode still open at full time is censored. Print its start and end frames and duration in frames. Any within-tick series retains call order. A claim replaces a prior episode only if one is still open; count such anomalies separately.

Record the first end cause as restart with cue, 360-frame engine drop, PASS/SHOOT/#10 or #11 kick at CONTACT, takeover/tackle, other explicit release, replacement claim, or full-time censor. Count keeper Decision Tree action selections while each episode is live, including PASS, SHOOT, DRIBBLE and HOLD; report last selected action with the end, but do not infer that a selected action caused the end absent actual CONTACT. Record #11 tactical state changes with raw caller frame, its stored `ClaimTick`, frame divided by six as the correctly normalized tactical tick, and elapsed physical frames from the claim. A state transition is not itself a physical release. Count all applied restart cues per seed; report simultaneous events in the event log rather than reassigning the first cause.

A same-keeper reclaim is a new hand or feet episode started at most **300 frames (five seconds)** after that keeper's own 360-frame ground drop, including frame 300 and excluding frame 301; count hand and feet separately. This is a descriptive window, not a suppression proposal. Track the last observed physical touch candidate from pass/shot/#10/#11 ApplyKick, first touch, and controlled pickup. The claim's preceding touch is captured **before** that claim updates the touch owner. A collision system deflection without an attributed actor sets `unattributed-collision-deflection`; a placed restart sets `restart-placement`; no prior observable touch is `unobserved`. Print agent and team when known, and count teammate deliberate-kick candidates separately from all same-team touches. A last-touch candidate does not establish an illegal back-pass; no legal inference from unattributed touches.

Count a keeper pass attempt at the actual #5 CONTACT, classified by the preceding hand or feet episode and receiver ID (negative means zone). Count receiver completion only when the next controlled acquisition names the committed receiver. A restart, other acquisition, new hand claim or another kick resolves the pending pass into its corresponding noncompletion bucket; a still-pending pass at full time is censored. This is a deliberately narrow contact-to-control measure; it does not infer #5's internal success flag from ball motion or call a receiverless zone pass a failure. Opening kickoff is a boot award, outside the `ApplyRestart` cue census.

Classify both keeper XY and ball contact XY at acquisition relative to own penalty area: team 0 goal line x=0, team 1 x=105, depth 16.5 m, lateral interval y=34 ± 20.16 m, inclusive boundaries. Report `inside`, `boundary`, and `outside` separately. These are diagnostic geometry constants pending the B/C normative spec and do not gate gameplay. Report hand claims per keeper and exposure, feet episode counts/durations, hand episode durations, guard firings and frame offsets, restart frequencies, acquisition and end causes, and the fraction of hand claims with unknown preceding touch. Compare the observed six-seed hand-claim count with the previously recorded W3 753 only after collecting A; a difference is a finding, not an automatic reason to discard A.

## Dry-run target-selection candidate

At each hand claim and feet acquisition, inspect the settled live roster without mutation: among nonsent-off **outfield teammates** within 25 m of keeper position (inclusive), choose minimum squared XY distance; an exact tie chooses the lower roster index. If none qualifies, label a receiverless fixed zone at (35,34) m for team 0 or (70,34) m for team 1. Print the policy currently carried by #21, candidate receiver ID or zone, distance and fallback frequency. This is a measurement-only candidate to assess selector feasibility, **not** approval of B's target range, delivery, geometry, or policy mapping. It consumes zero random draws and commits no intent.

## Fixed comparisons and falsifiers

This census is descriptive: no success threshold, target rate, empirical fit or post hoc seed selection. Preserve per-seed episodes and counts, not only pooled numbers. A baseline with missing claim attribution, claims outside the recorded geometry, early #11 transitions, keeper Decision Tree releases, or repeated claims is reported as found; do not retune A. Observer-on/off snapshot digests must match exactly for the same seed and frames. The A→B comparison reuses the same seeds, match setup, observer and per-claim definitions and reports any new unknown categories or source gaps. The helper refactor is separately tested for exact frozen-seed digest equality before it is accepted as behavior-neutral. A change in A corpus, sentinel, diagnostic implementation or seed coverage requires a named amended preregistration and a fresh A arm before interpretation.

The W8 run also reads the settled Tier-A ledger every tick and records foul, yellow, straight-red and second-yellow counts. Dispatch the **unchanged source-complete `foul-rate` instrument** separately on the same merged A commit and archive both artifacts together; its collision-candidate and force anatomy is complementary to this W8 ledger census. A disagreement in per-seed discipline totals is a source-gap finding to investigate before A→B.

Before B wiring, freeze its numeric policy mapping, delay and delivery bounds in the owning B specs. Predeclared B falsifiers are: a hand hold released solely by the old 360-frame drop in an ordinary seeded run; a duplicate launch or `DistributionExecutedEvent` for one claim; an event without a canonical #5 CONTACT; a keeper on-ball Decision Tree action racing a committed distribution; a receiver-targeted launch not reaching W5's pass feed; any receiverless launch creating a ghost W5 receiver; a missing-target episode failing to reach one canonical CONTACT before the old guard; a possession-loss episode launching after its teardown; and any inside-area hand episode surviving the earlier of the two old six-second clocks. These are structural falsifiers, not calibrated goal rates. For B's voluntary hold durations, require each ordinary successful hand distribution to have positive elapsed frames and CONTACT strictly before frame 360 from the claim; report the entire distribution and guard firings rather than fitting values to these matches. For A→B report every changed end reason and pass outcome per keeper; no pooled-only success rate.

Freeze the following boundary fixtures for B/C before implementation: no eligible receiver; last receiver sent off or removed before commit; receiver disappearing after commit and before CONTACT (last-known point clamped by F-09, falling back to the fixed zone only on the keeper's own goal line); one live canonical #5 CONTACT, no duplicate event, no ghost W5 receiver for a zone launch; mirrored keeper ends; and save/restore mid-windup. B fixtures include a commit before the inherited six-second deadline whose CONTACT would fall after it, proving earlier-clock cancellation, plus an injected mid-stride hand claim with no intent to expose the corrected #11 tactical boundary. C fixtures include hand control released exactly at 480 frames (legal), continuing to frame 481 (opponent corner **before** #5 CONTACT), feet possession beyond 480 frames (no hand offence), an own-area boundary at both ends, an outside-area contact (separate handling outcome, no eight-second corner), mirrored corner recipient and placement, and save/restore on either side of the deadline. For ordinary no-offence matches, B→C gameplay events and ball trajectories must match exactly per seed; report the first divergent frame and cause if they do not. Snapshot digests may diverge from C's serialized frame additions, so they are reported rather than used as the gameplay equality criterion. Rare sanction behavior is established by the forced fixtures, not by six matches.

The measurement is dispatched through `.github/workflows/measure.yml` with instrument `w8-stage-a`; its catalog entry fixes the env variable, test filter and report sentinel. CI without that env variable only compiles the instrument and runs the short observer neutrality check; it does not execute the full population. The artifact's TRX, instrument output and commit SHA are the evidence. No baseline figures are reviewed until this preregistration and its instrument are merged into main.
