## Comparison with the pre-fix Unity lane (inposs-platform-divergence-20261011, src 1839bb0f)
Five of six full traces are byte-identical to the pre-fix Unity traces (digests in that directory's
full-trace-sha256.txt), and those five shares match to the bit (unity-shares.tsv there):
0x00000000D1A6D05E, 0x00000000D1A6D05F, 0x1A2B3C4D5E6F7081, 0x5EED000000000003, 0x5EED000000000004.
So on those five Unity matches every recorded sample is unchanged by the fix (same sampling caveat
as the .NET half: an unsampled-tick difference that left every later sample unchanged is not excluded).

0x0F1E2D3C4B5A6978 is the seed that froze before the fix. Against the pre-fix trimmed Unity trace
(ticks -1..60 and every 600th tick from 3600) its rows are identical through sampled tick 56,400 and
first differ at checkpoint 57,000, the first checkpoint after the tick-56,956 post strike recorded in
stall-window-unity.txt. Pre-fix that checkpoint begins the frozen OutOfPlay run to 161,400; post-fix
the ball is in play there and no OutOfPlay row is sampled for the rest of the match. Its share moves
from 0.360611 (28,363 samples) to 0.949485 (16,708 samples), in line with the other five seeds
(0.939 to 0.971). This is the fix working on the governing build at the match that exposed the defect.
