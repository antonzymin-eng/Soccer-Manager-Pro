09 · G1 owner review checklist

decision surface

Accept only if these read as one System XI visual language.

The board clearly inherits the existing touchline analyst-tool direction rather than reopening stadium vs touchline.
The wordmark, icon, match, portrait, fictional-club, stadium, motion, and accessibility samples are specific enough that an off-style future sample would be recognizable and rejectable.
The match presentation looks capable of becoming professional Stage-1 presentation rather than placeholder-quality visuals.
Separately inspect Squad Screen.html, Tactics.html, and Club Finances.html at the 1920×1080 reference stage for the IBM Plex Sans Condensed T-01 substitution: no unacceptable clipping, wrapping, hierarchy loss, or density regression.
If all visual checks pass, record: “G1 accepted.” If not, identify the numbered board section or dense mockup screen and the visual defect.
