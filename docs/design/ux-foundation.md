## Historical decisions still retained by the active plans

The first UX memo established several useful principles that remain in force unless the active plans explicitly change them:

- preserve the chosen **`touchline`** analyst-tool visual direction rather than restarting visual design;
- treat `docs/design/ui-mockups/` as non-normative visual references;
- make the playable loop higher priority than polishing deep management surfaces;
- keep UI comprehension functional without final art;
- use stable asset slots and intentional fallbacks;
- keep localization expansion and accessibility constraints in the design process rather than at final polish;
- never let a mockup invent a simulation/view-model/command/navigation contract.

The original screen map, priority table, interaction-state inventory, art-slot table and first-slice ordering are **superseded** because they predated the dependency-grounded high-level/detailed planning and repeated portions now owned elsewhere.
