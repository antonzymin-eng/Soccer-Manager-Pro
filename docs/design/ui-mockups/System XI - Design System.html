01 / FOUNDATIONS

Philosophy

A management sim is read more than it is watched. The system optimizes for legibility at density 8/10, then earns drama back at match-day moments through display type and broadcast accents.

01 — Numbers first

Tabular numerals everywhere. The user is comparing 28 players across 30 attributes — alignment is content. Mono-spaced for stats; condensed sans for names.

02 — Quiet chrome, loud moments

UI surfaces stay neutral so league tables, scout reports, and inboxes don't compete. Match-day, signings, and trophies earn the broadcast treatment.

03 — One brand, club identity

Electric green is the game's voice — primary actions, focus, the manager's identity. Club crests and accent stripes carry team color into the chrome without overwhelming it.
